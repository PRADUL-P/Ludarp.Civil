## Reel → Carousel Conversion Note

Every post originally scripted as a Reel converts cleanly to carousel format like this:
- **Slide 1:** The problem ("Still doing X the slow way?")
- **Slide 2-4:** Step-by-step screenshots of the command in action
- **Final slide:** The payoff / time saved, plus a "save this" or "follow for more" call to action

This actually works well for a new account — carousels tend to get more saves than reels early on (saves > reach when you have zero followers, since saves signal quality to the algorithm even with small audiences).

## Notes for a Zero-Follower Account Specifically

1. **Consistency matters more than volume right now.** With no followers, the algorithm is testing your content on non-followers via Explore/Reels tab — carousels with strong hook slides (Slide 1) matter most for that first impression.
2. **First slide of every carousel is the only thing that gets shown before a swipe** — make sure each "Slide 1" in your graphics is a bold, standalone hook (e.g., "You're using EXPLODE wrong" rather than just a title like "Block Tips").
3. **Turn on your Business/Creator account before scheduling** — this unlocks Meta Business Suite scheduling and post insights, which you'll want from Day 1 to see what's actually landing.
4. Once you have ~20-30 posts live, revisit which ones got the most saves/shares and lean into that specific content type for Month 2 (Revit series).
